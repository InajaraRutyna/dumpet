# Dumpet på Måne Syv

A story game for practising Danish on a phone. Taken from your bed, changed halfway, dumped on a moon where every word is magic — and every word is Danish.

Play: https://inajararutyna.github.io/dumpet/

- Everything is drawn and composed in code: no images, no recordings.
- Progress is saved on the device. Leaving mid-scene resumes at the same step.
- Tap any Danish word to see what it means. Answer by tapping words, writing, or speaking (where the browser supports it).
- The earlier version of the game is kept in `old/`.

Fonts: Space Grotesk and Fraunces, under the SIL Open Font License.
